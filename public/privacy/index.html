Privacy policy

Short version: we don't track you, and what you type into our tools stays on your device.

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

Who we are

Creator Signals (creatorsignals.blog) is an independent educational platform for content creators. Questions about privacy: hello@creatorsignals.blog.

What we do not do

  • We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.
  • We do not use analytics tools or social media pixels.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • Fonts are hosted on our own domain, so no data is sent to font providers.

Data in your browser (local storage)

The Hook Builder, Content Planner and sample lesson save your input in your browser's local storage so you can continue later. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it with the “Reset” or “Clear my progress” buttons, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Hosting

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver and protect the website, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses and request information in server logs. This is necessary to run a secure website (legitimate interest).

Newsletter

The newsletter is not active yet and no email addresses are collected. Before it launches, this section will describe the email provider we use, what we store (your email address and proof of your consent), how long we keep it, and how to unsubscribe. You will always be able to unsubscribe with one click.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
