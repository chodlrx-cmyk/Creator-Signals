FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Creator Signals affiliated with TikTok?

No. Creator Signals is an independent educational platform. TikTok is a trademark of its owner and is mentioned only to describe what we teach.

I'm a complete beginner. Where do I start?

Download the free Creator Starter Kit, then try the sample lesson and the Content Planner. If you like the format, the 30-Day Creator Launchpad is built exactly for you.

Will I go viral or earn money?

We can't promise that, and nobody honestly can. We teach skills and a system for making better decisions about your content — results depend on many factors.

When can I buy the programmes?

Only when they're fully finished. The prices shown are planned launch prices, not discounts on an earlier price.

Where is my planner data stored?

Only in your own browser on this device. We don't receive it. Use “Export” in the planner to make a backup or move to another device.

Does this work in my country?

Built for creators worldwide. Creator Signals provides practical learning resources for beginners and growing creators around the world. TikTok features and tools are primarily checked using a UK account. Availability, names and requirements may vary by country, account type and app version. When in doubt, your own app is right.

What language are the programmes in?

Simple, accessible English (British spelling), so non-native speakers can follow along easily.
