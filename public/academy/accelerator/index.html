Questions

About the Accelerator.

Should I do the Launchpad first?

The Accelerator is for creators who have finished the basics: you know your niche, you have posted videos and you can read your analytics. If you are just starting, begin with the 30-Day Creator Launchpad.

How is the 90 days organised?

Three phases of 30 days, twelve weekly modules and three milestones. Each learning week has five lesson days, a publish & weekly review day and a rest or catch-up day. A full lesson day takes about 45–65 minutes (a 15–20 minute lesson plus the exercise); the ★ short route for busy days takes about 20–30 minutes.

What do I get exactly?

One download (ZIP): the Complete Course Book (PDF, 480 pages, also as 15 separate parts), the Interactive Workbook (PDF, 178 pages), the Creator OS spreadsheet (12 sheets), the printable 90-Day Planner (PDF, 49 pages), Portfolio & Media Kit Templates, the Creator Growth Portfolio, a fictional creator's complete 90-day example and a Start Here guide. It is not a video course.

Which apps do I need?

Adobe Acrobat Reader is recommended for the fillable PDFs. The Creator OS was tested in Excel for the web and Google Sheets; the presentation templates in PowerPoint for the web and Google Slides.

Will it help me earn money?

It teaches monetisation fundamentals — brand deals, UGC, rates, a portfolio and a media kit — but no one can honestly promise income, views or followers. You make decisions with your own data.

What if it isn't for me?

You have a 14-day money-back guarantee: email hello@creatorsignals.blog within 14 days of your purchase for a full refund. See the terms of sale.

Is Creator Signals affiliated with TikTok?

No. Creator Signals is an independent educational platform. The course is written in British English for creators worldwide; TikTok features were checked with a UK account in November 2026 and can differ by country, account type and app version.
