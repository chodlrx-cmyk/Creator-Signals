          Free Kit clicks—Links to the Starter Kit

          Article CTA CTR—CTA clicks ÷ article views

          How these numbers work. Views = page loads of the article (cookieless; bots and your own signed-in admin browser are not counted; links tagged utm_source=test are left out). Free Kit / 30-Day / 90-Day = clicks on the article's links to /starter-kit/, /academy/launchpad/ and /academy/accelerator/, counted from 17 Nov 2026. CTR = (Free Kit + 30-Day + 90-Day clicks) ÷ views in the same period and source. A click is not a sale. Orders and revenue are not attributable to articles: Payhip orders carry no link to a visit. Google / Bing / TikTok = views arriving from that site (or tagged with that utm_source). Bing is counted separately from 17 Nov 2026; earlier Bing visits sit under “Other”.
