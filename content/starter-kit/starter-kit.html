01 · Learn

How discovery works —
the honest version.

Nobody outside TikTok knows the exact formula, and it changes over time. Anyone who promises “the secret algorithm hack” is guessing. What we do know is enough to start well.

What TikTok says it looks at

In its own public explanations, TikTok says the For You feed is personalised using signals such as:

  • How people interact with videos — for example watching, rewatching, liking, sharing, commenting or skipping.
  • Information in the video — such as captions, sounds and topics.
  • Settings — like language and country, which help decide who might be interested.

Check TikTok's own Creator Academy and help pages for the latest details — platforms update their explanations regularly.

What this means for you

Focus on

  • A clear opening that tells viewers why to stay
  • One topic per video
  • Content for a specific audience
  • Consistency you can keep up

Ignore

  • “Magic” hashtags that guarantee reach
  • One perfect posting time for everyone
  • Shadowban rumours without evidence
  • Promises of going viral
Signal, not proof. One video doing well (or badly) tells you very little. Patterns across several videos tell you a lot. That's why this kit makes you track 7.

01 · Learn

10 hook formulas.

A hook is the first line or moment of your video. It answers the viewer's silent question: “why should I keep watching?” Replace [topic] and [audience] with your own.

Mistake“3 mistakes [audience] make with [topic] — and how to fix them.”
How-to“How to start with [topic], even as a complete beginner.”
Result-first“I tried [topic] for 7 days. Here's what changed.”
Question“Is [topic] really worth it for [audience]?”
Curiosity“The one tip about [topic] I wish I'd known sooner.”
Bold claim“[Topic] is simpler than you think.”
List“3 things every beginner should know about [topic].”
Story“The day [topic] finally clicked for me.”
Save this“Save this: a simple checklist for [topic].”
Contrast“Everyone says this about [topic]. Here's what I do instead.”
Make the hook true. The rest of the video must deliver what the hook promises. Clickbait that doesn't deliver makes people leave — and stop trusting you.

Simple video
script.

Most short educational videos work well with this five-part structure. Keep each part short — say it out loud and cut what you don't need.

1

Hook — the first line. Why should they stay?

2

Context — who is this for, what problem?

3

Value — the tip, steps or story (the main part).

4

Proof or example — show it, demonstrate it, give an example.

5

Ending — a simple next step (“save this”, “try it today”, a question for comments).

04 · Analyze

Analytics basics.

TikTok shows analytics for your videos in the app (the exact names and location can change with app updates). These are the numbers worth understanding first.

Views

How many times the video was played. Shows reach — not whether people liked it.

Average watch time

How long people watched on average. Compare it with the length of your video.

Watched full video / retention

How many viewers stayed until the end, and where they dropped off. A big drop at the start often means the hook can be clearer.

Likes, comments, shares, saves

Shares and saves often mean the content was useful enough to keep or pass on.

New followers

How many people followed after this video — a sign they want more of the same.

Traffic source

Where views came from (e.g. For You, search, your profile). Search views mean your topic and caption match what people look for.

Engagement rate = (likes + comments + shares) ÷ views × 100%One common definition. Others exist — always use the same one when you compare your own videos.
